## 2. What Data We Collect

### Data We Do NOT Collect
- Your browsing history
- DNS queries made through the VPN tunnel
- The contents of your network traffic
- Your precise location
- Your contacts, photos, or files
- Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID)
- Personally identifiable information beyond what you voluntarily provide

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device Only
- WireGuard server profiles you import (including public keys and endpoints)
- Your connection preferences (selected server, auto-connect settings)
- App settings (theme preference, diagnostics logs)

This data is stored in Android SharedPreferences on your device. It is not transmitted to Vidonex or any third party.

### Data We May Collect If You Contact Support
- Your email address (if you email us)
- Description of your issue and any diagnostic information you voluntarily share
- App version and Android version you report

## 4. Third-Party Services

**The app itself does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.**

If you connect through a WireGuard server, that server is operated by you or a third party of your choosing. We have no control over third-party server operators' data practices. Please review their privacy policies.

## 5. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:
- WireGuard private keys are never logged or transmitted
- App data backup is disabled (allowBackup=false)
- All VPN traffic is encrypted by the WireGuard protocol

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.
